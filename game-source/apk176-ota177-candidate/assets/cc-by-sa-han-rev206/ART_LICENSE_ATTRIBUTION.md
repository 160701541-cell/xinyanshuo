## Authenticity and quality
The ancient Han meshes show culturally appropriate roof forms; they are not claims of literal historical architectural accuracy or finished housing interiors. Godot collision, access to interiors, material assignment and five-person shelter allocation are OUR gameplay responsibility, checked separately. A genuine but untextured model is NOT a visually accepted game model.
